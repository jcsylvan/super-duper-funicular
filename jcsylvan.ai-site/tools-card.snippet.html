Document Marking Scanner → Point it at a folder of PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and text files and it comes back solved like a Rubik's cube: one row per file, linked to the file, saying whether the document carries an export control warning (ITAR/EAR) or a CUI marking and which US government contract number it cites. Headers, footers, slide notes and spreadsheet headers are read too, and scanned pages go through OCR. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
